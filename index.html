Questions

How far ahead should I book?

Please book at least 2 days in advance, and two weeks or more for holidays and house sitting. Requests made within 24 hours are handled as availability allows and carry an additional $5 squeeze-in fee.

What happens at the meet and greet?

It’s a free 20–30 minute visit at your home. Your dog meets Sydney, you share feeding, leash and house details, and you confirm the plan together.

Do you take puppies, seniors or anxious dogs?

Yes. Sydney has fostered puppies as young as 8 weeks and is comfortable with seniors, shy dogs and high-energy breeds. Share details in your request so the plan fits.

How will I know how the visit went?

You’ll get a short note after each visit, with a photo when possible.

How do I pay?

Payment options include Venmo, Zelle, Cash App and card, and are shared when your booking is confirmed.
